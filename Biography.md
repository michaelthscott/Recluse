# MICHAEL SCOTT

Nasceu em Ballyaughlis, Irlanda do Norte, 1954.

Estudou na Chelsea School of Art, Londres.\
BA e MA Fine Art Degrees, 1977-1981.

| 1985 | "British Artists", Art Palace, Nova Iorque. |
| 1987 | John Moores Exhibition 15, Walker Art Gallery, Liverpool. |
| 1991 | Galeria Atlântica. ART LONDON '91. Londres. |
